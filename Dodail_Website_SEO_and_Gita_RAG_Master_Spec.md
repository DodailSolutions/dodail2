## 1. Instructions to Claude: how to use this document

Build the website using this document as the content and SEO
specification. Do not simply paste every section into one page. Create
the pages in the sitemap, implement the navigation and internal links,
and use the page-specific copy/metadata below.

### Non-negotiable rules

1.  Do not invent customer names, reviews, certifications, office
    locations, employee counts, project outcomes, performance
    percentages, awards, pricing, guarantees, or product availability.
2.  Do not publish "100+ clients," "100+ case studies," "14+ years of
    expertise," or numeric growth claims unless the business owner
    supplies evidence and approves the exact wording. Existing public
    copy contains some of these claims, but they are not independently
    verified in this brief.
3.  Treat customer logos, testimonials, portfolio screenshots and case
    studies as proof assets. Reuse them only if Dodail has permission
    and can confirm the work and the claims.
4.  Keep "GEO" unambiguous. In this website, use **Generative Engine
    Optimization (GEO)** when discussing AI-search visibility. Use
    **geographic expansion** only when that is what the service means.
    Do not label both services with the same acronym.
5.  Do not call Dodail "the best" or promise first-place rankings,
    guaranteed leads, guaranteed revenue, or guaranteed AI-search
    citations.
6.  Use a human, specific, business-focused tone. Avoid generic filler
    such as "revolutionary," "cutting-edge," "unlock your potential,"
    and "in today's digital landscape."
7.  Build accessible, responsive, fast pages with server-rendered or
    statically rendered content where appropriate. Search engines must
    be able to read the main copy without clicking, scrolling-triggered
    network calls, or executing complex animations.
8.  Use one clear H1 per page, logical H2/H3 hierarchy, descriptive
    links, breadcrumbs on nested pages, canonical URLs, unique metadata
    and an XML sitemap.
9.  Never create thin near-duplicate pages just to target every
    keyword/city. Each indexable page must answer a distinct search
    intent with useful, original content.
10. Use a central content model/CMS or structured data files so the same
    service facts can feed the website and Gita's RAG without drifting.

### Implementation order

-   Phase 1: technical foundation, global navigation/footer, Home,
    About, Contact, Services index.
-   Phase 2: core service pages (AI automation, custom software,
    websites, e-commerce, SEO/GEO, digital marketing, branding/UI/UX).
-   Phase 3: industry solution pages, portfolio, case studies, products.
-   Phase 4: blog/resource hub, FAQ library, legal pages, redirects and
    migration QA.
-   Do not publish empty or placeholder pages. If content is not ready,
    noindex the draft or leave the route unpublished.

### Keyword targeting principles

-   Use one primary intent per URL and a small set of closely related
    secondary terms.
-   Use "website development company in Hyderabad" naturally on the
    website development page, not in every page title.
-   Use "digital marketing agency in Hyderabad" on the relevant
    service/landing page with evidence of local service and contact
    details.
-   Do not create USA, UK, Dubai and India pages with the same copy and
    only swapped place names. Create regional pages only when there is a
    genuinely different offer, audience, currency, legal context, proof
    or service model.
-   "Best" and "top" are user search modifiers, not claims Dodail should
    make about itself.
-   Use natural variations in headings and copy; do not publish a
    visible keyword list.

A person submits a form, messages your business or requests a callback.

**2. The request is organized**\
The system captures relevant information, applies your agreed rules and
routes the request to the right person or queue.

**3. The next action happens**\
Where integrations and permissions allow, a workflow can update records,
notify staff, schedule follow-up or send an approved response.

**4. Your team can see what happened**\
Records and reporting make it easier to understand response status,

This is an example workflow, not a claim that every integration is
included by default.

of success.\
**Build** --- Design, develop, integrate and test the solution.\

improvements.

## 10. SEO technical implementation checklist

### Crawl and indexation

-   Crawl the current site before changing it; export every URL, status
    code, title, H1, canonical, indexability, inbound links and image
    alt text.
-   Review Google Search Console and analytics before deciding what to
    remove.
-   Ensure each intended page returns 200 and is indexable.
-   Use 301 redirects for retired URLs with a genuine equivalent.
-   Return 404/410 for removed spam pages without a relevant
    replacement.
-   Avoid redirect chains, loops, soft 404s and redirecting unrelated
    URLs to the homepage.
-   Generate XML sitemap(s) containing only canonical, indexable,
    200-status URLs.
-   Add and verify `robots.txt`; do not block CSS/JS needed for
    rendering.
-   Do not place noindex pages in the sitemap.
-   Canonical tags must point to the preferred URL.
-   Ensure staging sites are password-protected or noindexed and cannot
    leak into search.

### On-page SEO

-   Unique title and meta description for each indexable page.
-   One clear H1; structured H2/H3 hierarchy.
-   Human-readable, descriptive URLs.
-   Useful body copy that answers the intended search query.
-   Contextual internal links and breadcrumbs.
-   Descriptive image filenames and meaningful alt text; decorative
    images use empty alt.
-   Open Graph and social share metadata.
-   Avoid duplicate title tags, empty headings and repeated boilerplate
    blocks.
-   No hidden keyword text, cloaking or doorway pages.

### Structured data

Implement only types supported by the visible page and actual business
facts: - `Organization` or appropriate
`ProfessionalService`/`LocalBusiness` schema for Dodail, after canonical
business details are confirmed. - `WebSite` and `BreadcrumbList` where
appropriate. - `Service` only if useful and accurate; it does not
guarantee rich results. - `Article`/`BlogPosting` for genuine
articles. - `Product` only for real products with accurate availability
and offer information. - `Review`/`AggregateRating` only where compliant
with Google's rules; never self-generate fake ratings or mark up
testimonials that do not qualify. - Do not add FAQ rich-result markup
expecting Google to display FAQ snippets for a commercial website; use
FAQ content for users and RAG, with markup only when appropriate to
current guidelines. - Validate with Google Rich Results Test and Schema
Markup Validator.

### Performance and accessibility

-   Target good Core Web Vitals: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1 at
    the 75th percentile where field data is available.
-   Compress images, use modern formats, responsive sizes and lazy
    loading below the fold.
-   Do not lazy-load the main LCP hero image.
-   Minimize blocking scripts, third-party widgets and unused CSS.
-   Use semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient
    contrast and labelled form controls.
-   Respect reduced-motion preferences.
-   Test mobile first, with sticky widgets and chat controls not
    covering content.
-   Optimize fonts and avoid layout shifts.
-   Test with Lighthouse/PageSpeed Insights and real devices.

### Security, privacy and trust

-   HTTPS across all URLs and assets.
-   Secure headers and CMS/admin access; patch dependencies.
-   Rate-limit and validate form endpoints; use server-side secrets.
-   Publish accurate privacy policy and terms.
-   Document cookies/analytics and obtain consent where legally
    required.
-   Do not send sensitive form data to analytics tools.
-   Make contact details, legal identity and company information
    consistent.

### Analytics and conversion measurement

Track meaningful events with consent and privacy controls: -
`contact_form_start` - `contact_form_submit` -
`book_consultation_click` - `click_email` - `click_phone` -
`whatsapp_click` - `service_cta_click` - `case_study_view` -
`download_resource` (if downloads exist)

Use UTM conventions for campaigns. Define a qualified enquiry with the
business owner. Separate leads, qualified leads, meetings and sales; do
not report all form submissions as revenue.
